Shilo Preshyon is a producer, composer, recording artist, performer, multi-instrumentalist, and sound engineer based in Stz’uminus/Yellow Point, BC. Shilo is fluent with Pro Tools, Sound Forge, recording equipment & processes, analog and digital rack gear, tape machines, samplers, MIDI controllers, and audio plugins. Shilo Preshyon is a Vancouver Island–based musician, artist, producer, and audio engineer of mixed heritage. A dedicated community builder, they connect artists across the West Coast through collaboration and mentorship. Their work reflects the natural and social environments that surround them, drawing inspiration from found materials, technology, and the intersection of art, ecology, and healing.

Primarily self-taught in music, recording, and sound design, Shilo approaches their craft with curiosity and experimentation. Their background in community mental health and support work, including time at the Portland Hotel Society and the Garth Homer Society, informs their practice with empathy and a deep commitment to accessibility and inclusion in the arts.

Living with chronic migraines, autoimmune disorders, and PTSD, Shilo channels personal challenges into creative expression and connection, using their art to explore resilience and grounding in the contemporary world. They are currently the head engineer and host of Risqué Disque Studios, an artist-run recording and residency space in Yellow Point, BC, where they prioritize mentorship and recording opportunities for underrepresented artists and emerging engineers. They are also a founding member of the artist collectives Ba’as, a small sounder, Soup du Jour, and Tasty Palm Leaf.
